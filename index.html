Meet your stylist

Hi, I’m Ya Ya.

I’m a loc stylist with a real passion for what locs can become — and for the person wearing them.

Every appointment is about transforming your locs and enhancing the natural beauty you already carry, through styling and care that’s personalized to you: your hair, your goals, your vibe.

Whether you’re keeping it neat with a fresh retwist or ready to switch it up with something new, your hair journey is my top priority. Always.

  • i.

    Personalized care

    Every visit is shaped around your locs and what you want from them.

  • ii.

    Healthy maintenance

    Fresh roots and neat parts that keep your locs looking their best.

  • iii.

    Creative styling

    From braids to barrels to updos — styles that let you show up as you.

The Lovely Locs promise

Locs are self-expression. My mission is to help you show off your unique beauty through stunning loc styles — so every transformation leaves you with more confidence, more creativity, and a look that feels completely, unmistakably you.
